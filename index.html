? HOW TO PLAY

1. EXPLORE. Trees near you appear on the map. Walk to them IRL for full effect.

2. NAME IT. Tap a tree, get 60 SECONDS, and type any name you like — a real species, or "Steve". Quick-pick chips are just suggestions.

3. THE CROWD IS THE JUDGE — LIKE WIKIPEDIA. Votes are secret until you cast yours, and each vote carries the voter's TRUST WEIGHT. A name is CONFIRMED once it holds 60%+ of the weight. "Steve" can win early — but as trusted namers pile onto the real species, the name flips and the tree is publicly renamed. Truth wins over time.

4. EARN TRUST (REP). Everyone starts a SPROUT (×1). Call an unconfirmed tree right and the crowd later agrees → +8 REP. Guess wrong, or plant a name that gets overturned → rep down. Climb: SCOUT ×1.5 → RANGER ×2 → ARBORIST ×3 → TREE ELDER ×5. Higher trust = heavier votes.

5. DISCOVER. Science knows 60,000+ tree species — ~9,000 still undiscovered. Confirmed names that match no known species become candidates in your Treedex. Maybe you'll surface a real one.
